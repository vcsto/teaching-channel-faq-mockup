Can I Add More Users?

Yes, you are able to update your list of users.

There are two ways in which you are able to initiate in adding users to your user list:

  1. Your Client Success Manager will be able to do this for you.
  2. If you'd like to do so, please speak with your Client Success Manager for extra training for this right.
